Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account we collect your name and email address. When you use Spendly we also store the expense details you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to run the service: to sign you in, to save and display your expenses, and to show you summaries of your spending. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in the Spendly database and kept for as long as your account is active. We take reasonable steps to protect it, but no system is completely secure. You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes requests to Google's servers when you visit our pages. We do not share your expense data with any third party.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or want your data deleted, contact us at support@example.com.
